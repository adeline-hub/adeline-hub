Project Objective

To design and implement a scalable framework for integrating IoT sensors and sustainable energy grids into new commercial real estate developments, ensuring compliance with ISO 37122 (Indicators for Smart Cities).

Project Description

This initiative required coordinating between hardware vendors, software engineering teams, and urban planners. The project was executed in phases: Initiation (Feasibility & Charter), Planning (WBS & Risk Register), Execution (Vendor Integration), Monitoring (KPI tracking via dashboards), and Closing (Handover to operations).

Products & Technologies

Jira, MS Project, PowerBI, AutoCAD, IoT Middleware APIs, AWS IoT Core.

Project Wins

  • Delivered the project 3 weeks ahead of the baseline schedule.
  • Maintained Cost Performance Index (CPI) at 1.05 (Under budget).
  • Successfully onboarded 3 distinct third-party hardware vendors without scope creep.

Lessons Learned

  • Vendor Lead Times: Global supply chain issues caused hardware delays. Future projects will require a 20% time buffer in procurement phases.
  • Stakeholder Alignment: Early integration of the municipal compliance team prevented costly rework during the quality audit phase.
