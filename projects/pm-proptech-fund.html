Project Objective

Develop an investor-ready venture capital fund model in the PropTech sector in France through a compliant financial engineering framework, jointly structured between a financial engineering company and a PropTech incubator. The assignment focused on transforming an investment thesis into a credible fund architecture with governance, operational logic, lifecycle clarity, and execution discipline suitable for presentation to prospective investors and strategic partners.

Core Mandate

The mandate was to create a robust venture capital fund framework capable of being presented to external investors with confidence. This meant bridging strategic ambition and execution discipline: translating an opportunity in proptech innovation into a structured fund vehicle with clear operating rules, risk boundaries, decision rights, and financial lifecycle consistency from fund launch through portfolio realization.

Investment Thesis Design: Defined the strategic rationale of the fund, including target market logic, portfolio orientation, value creation assumptions, and alignment between sector opportunity and investor expectations.
Fund Architecture: Structured the operating backbone of the fund across governance, decision flows, capital allocation principles, portfolio review mechanisms, and lifecycle checkpoints.
External Investor Positioning: Developed a framework that could support due diligence discussions by making the fund understandable, coherent, and financially disciplined before active investor solicitation.

Scope of Work

  • Lifecycle Model: Mapped the full fund lifecycle from structuring and launch through sourcing, screening, investment, portfolio support, reporting, and exit realization.
  • Financial Engineering: Built the financial logic supporting fund mechanics, including allocation principles, operating assumptions, and performance framing suitable for investor engagement.
  • Compliance Framing: Ensured the project evolved within a financially compliant framework so the fund proposition could be advanced with stronger institutional credibility.
  • Investor Readiness: Organized the structure, language, and governance narrative required to make the initiative presentable to external capital partners and strategic backers.

Key Outcomes and Strategic Value

  • Investor-Ready Fund Model: Transformed an initial investment thesis into a structured venture capital framework with clearer architecture, governance logic, and operational coherence.
  • Governance-Driven Credibility: Reinforced the attractiveness of the project by clarifying decision rights, role boundaries, oversight mechanisms, and lifecycle controls expected by sophisticated investors.
  • Operational Clarity Across the Fund Lifecycle: Moved beyond concept-stage positioning by defining how the fund would function in practice from sourcing and investment review to portfolio support and exit preparation.
  • Strategic Ecosystem Integration: Strengthened the market relevance of the vehicle by combining financial engineering capability with incubator-driven PropTech access, creating a more differentiated and credible platform.

PMI-Oriented Delivery Sensibility

  • Integration: Balanced financial, legal, strategic, and ecosystem inputs into a single coherent development pathway.
  • Risk and Governance: Treated compliance, oversight, and decision rights as foundational design variables rather than supporting documents.
  • Stakeholder Management: Maintained alignment across organizations with different priorities, time horizons, and definitions of success.

Lessons Learned

  • Investment Theses Need Operating Logic: A compelling thesis alone is not enough. Investors require clarity on how a fund will actually function, make decisions, govern risk, and create value over time.
  • Governance Is Part of Product Design: In fund structuring, governance should not be treated as an administrative layer added at the end. It is central to the credibility and investability of the vehicle itself.
  • Ecosystem Access Must Be Structurally Embedded: The strategic value of an incubator partnership becomes more credible when it is translated into sourcing, screening, and portfolio support mechanisms within the fund model.
  • Compliance-Aware Structuring Improves Confidence: Integrating regulatory and financial engineering considerations early helps create a stronger basis for investor dialogue and reduces ambiguity in execution expectations.
